01 — FEATURES

Make your tools while you paint.

Most paint apps stop at letting you pick a brush. Nishikie Lab lets you reach inside it.

ONE STROKE, ONE DISTANCE FIELDTextures follow the line, however it bends.

Instead of a row of stamps, each stroke is treated as a single distance field. No seams at corners — the texture flows along the line.

Pressure-sensitive, with natural tapering, stabilizer, color mixing and dry-brush streaks.

EVERY BRUSH IS CODE YOU CAN READChange one number, get a new brush.

Open any built-in brush as GLSL. Drag the sliders or rewrite the code, then save it as your own and keep it on a live-thumbnail shelf.

ASK AN AI FOR A BRUSHAsk. Paste. Paint.

Copy the built-in prompt template into your favorite AI chat and describe the brush you want. Paste the code it returns and you have a working brush or material — no coding required.

BRUSH PICKERPick a brush by what it paints.

The list shows a real stroke painted with each brush: sweeping ink, dry brush, soft-edge, multi-line, gold leaf, halftone… The feel changes the moment you pick one.

Save your slider settings under a brush name, sort them with favorites and shelves, and export them as .nlbrush files to share. Long-press the brush tool to reach your favorites.

TEXTURE BRUSHESTurn a painting into a brush.

Drag a layer into the texture slot and it becomes the brush texture. Ribbon runs it along the stroke, Stamp places it one by one, Tile fills an area with the pattern.

Angle, range, mirroring and end caps are all adjustable — lace, chains, garlands and decorative borders in a single stroke.

VECTOR LINE ARTWarp it, and the lines stay lines.

Run the Warp tool over a folder and its vector line-art layers bend together with the paint. The vector data survives the warp, so you can keep moving points to fix the line.

Line width and brush shape can be swapped after the fact too — tilt a face or rework the flow of the hair without redrawing a single line.

LAYER MATERIALSEffects you can change later.

Halftone, outline, paper grain, Voronoi dissolve, pseudo-lighting — swap a layer's whole look with a shader. Non-destructive, all numbers, adjustable any time.

COLOR MIXING — LIKE PAINTYellow plus blue makes green.

Color mixing picks up paint under the brush as you drag, and you choose how it mixes. Digital blends RGB, like light. Pigment mixes reflectance spectra (Kubelka–Munk), so yellow + blue is green, red + blue is purple, and mixtures stay vivid instead of going muddy.

Works with every brush, and can pick up colors from the layers below.

DEPTH & LIGHTGive a flat painting real relief.

Add depth to a layer and light it in 3D. Move the light and the shading shifts like thick paint on canvas.

Depth can come from the painting's brightness or be painted directly, and the relief can be baked out as a normal map layer — handy for 3D textures. The Light tool lets you move and tilt the light after the fact.

UI THEMESYour toolbox, your look.

The default is “Sakura washi” — paper with a touch of cherry pink. There are also Dark, Light and “Nishiki”, whose washi-and-pattern backdrop is drawn by a shader. Accent and heading colors are yours to change, and you can write the UI backdrop itself in GLSL.

03 — IN THE WORKS

On the workbenchIN DEVELOPMENT

Nishikie Lab gets worked on almost every day. Here is a peek at what is coming next.

MOBILE EDITIONOn phones and tablets.

The same engine and the same brushes, rebuilt for fingers and pens. Tools sit within reach of one hand, and the dock can be moved to any edge of the screen. The goal is to bring the brushes and materials you made on desktop with you.

WATERCOLOR BRUSHIt bleeds, it blends, it dries with an edge.

A watercolor brush that simulates water and pigment moving on paper — available since 1.06 (and still growing). A loaded wash spreads with ragged edges, mingles with its neighbors without turning muddy, and dries with a dark rim. Drop water into a wet wash to push the color out, or blow on it to scatter it into streaks.

Once dry it becomes an ordinary painted layer, so it combines freely with the other brushes and materials.

Screens, features and timing may change while in development. Follow @nao3art on X for news.

07 — FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. Double-click the included HTML file and it opens in your browser, ready to paint. A launcher that opens it in a tab-less “app window” is included too.

What is the difference between the free and full editions?

Painting features are identical. The free edition cannot save projects, export PNG/PSD or keep automatic backups. You can still copy the image to the clipboard and paste it anywhere.

Do I need a graphics card?

No. Integrated GPUs from 2012 onward work fine. You need a browser with WebGL2 — run the compatibility check if you're unsure.

Is my artwork sent anywhere?

No. By default the app never connects to the internet. No ads, no account, no telemetry.

Can I use my artwork commercially?

Yes. Artwork you create is entirely yours. Redistributing the app itself is not permitted.

Are updates free?

Yes. 1.x updates are free for existing customers — just download the latest file again.

Where do I send bug reports or requests?

Mention or DM @nao3art on X. The developer reads every message. Attaching your compatibility-check result helps a lot.
